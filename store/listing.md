## Short description (80)

Iraqi student app: ministry news, study timer with 40 themes, study room & tools.

## Full description (English)

Academy 6 (أكـادمي السادس) is a free app made for Iraqi students, especially the 6th preparatory grade.

- Ministry of Education news: exams, results, admissions and official holidays, with a holiday calendar for every governorate.
- Study timer: Pomodoro and other known presets (50/10, 52/17, 90/20) or your own times, a free countdown and a stopwatch, with 40 high-resolution themes (night, nature, Iraq, space, calm colors) that fit any screen.
- Our Room: a 3D study room with cute anime characters, a shared timer and chat.
- My Expenses: wallet, budget and savings goals, watched by the AI tutor.
- 30+ tools: AI tutor, students' forum, weekly timetable, mistakes notebook, flashcards, live duels, points store, Iraq forest, anonymous vent, calm-before-exams, and more.
- Safe community: clear rules, report and block buttons, and moderators who review reports. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

The app is not a government service. Please check official sources for dates and decisions.
